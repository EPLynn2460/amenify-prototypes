How to use the prototype

1
Start on screen 1 (Home). Tap Make a Payment to walk the rent flow (screens 2–8).
2
On screen 6 (Approved) or screen 8 (Resident Portal Wallet), tap the lavender "Link a card to earn everywhere" banner to enter the spend flow (screens 9–12).
3
Tap anywhere on the phone to highlight clickable areas, or use ← / → arrow keys to step through all twelve screens.
