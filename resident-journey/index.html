    FAQs

    Answers for everyone who builds the journey.

    For property management companies

    Happier residents, more renewals, and no new app to roll out.

    Book a demo
    Do residents need to download a new app?

    No. Shop Local lives inside the resident apps and portals you already use, through your property management software partner. Residents get it where they already pay rent.

    Where is Shop Local available?

    Shop Local serves residents in 1,000+ cities nationwide, with local pros and merchants in each market.

    Who makes sure services are done well?

    Atlas Engine sends vetted pros, every job is tracked from booking to done, and a live concierge team checks quality. Residents have left more than 150,000 five-star reviews.

    Can we tailor it to each community?

    Yes. Services, renewal gifts and Credit Drops can be set for each property. For example, a $100 renewal gift residents can use with their own housekeepers.

    Can we send gifts automatically?

    Yes, and every gift is optional. Set a welcome gift for new leases, a renewal gift, or a small make-good (like $10) that goes out automatically when a maintenance request closes. You choose which gifts are on and how much each one is.

    Can residents get help furnishing before move-in?

    Yes. Share your floor plans and residents get a free 3D design of their unit, furnished to their style, and can buy furniture direct from suppliers at a discount.

    How does this help retention?

    Residents earn back on rent and everyday spending, get a gift when they renew, and have a concierge who handles life at home. All of that gives them reasons to stay.

    For property management software partners

    More engagement and a new revenue driver for your core systems.

    Learn more
    What does Shop Local do for our platform?

    Partners see a 45% increase in monthly active engagement. Earning back on rent and everyday spending gives residents a reason to open your app every week, not just on rent day.

    How does it integrate?

    We embed through robust APIs and plugins, with developer support throughout. Shop Local runs inside your resident experience under your platform.

    How does it drive revenue for us?

    Shop Local turns resident engagement into a revenue driver for your core systems. On-time rent rewards bring payments into your platform, and commerce and services add new income.

    Do we need to build the services or the merchant network?

    No. Maddie AI, Atlas Engine dispatch, Visa-linked offers and the merchant network all come ready to use.

    For merchants

    Reach renters where they live, and pay only for results.

    Partner with us
    Who can I reach?

    Residents in 24M+ homes across 1,000+ cities, targeted by the personas you want: movers-in, families, commuters, pet owners and more.

    How do offers work for residents?

    Offers are card-linked to the Visa cards residents already carry. Residents earn up to 5% back with no codes, coupons or receipts, and it stacks with their card’s own rewards.

    Where does my brand show up?

    In rent rewards, the resident app, Maddie AI recommendations and across the Visa network, at the moments residents are ready to buy.

    What does a category win look like?

    Take CVS. A welcome reward sets CVS as the resident’s default pharmacy. A linked Visa card adds an instant reason to shop there. Maddie offers pickup or delivery when the first prescription is ready. Refills and 2% rewards turn that into a habit and recurring revenue.

    How do I pay, and what results can I expect?

    Pricing is based on performance: you pay for incremental sales above a control group. In a 12-week test, merchants saw +43% traffic, +28% basket size and +67% repeat visits.
