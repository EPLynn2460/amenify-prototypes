Design notes

The rmResident flow mirrors the LOFT and Homebody structures (eight screens, Visa happy path, ACH alternate path, rewards wallet endpoint) and rebrands every reward surface in rmResident's own visual language — navy + orange, geometric diamond patterns, utilitarian sans-serif typography, and a property-management aesthetic pulled directly from the rmResident App Store screens.

Triple reinforcement of the reward

The $21.05 + 8,420 points figure appears three times: in the inline offer on Payment Method, in the Summary callout, and again in the Approved screen. Followed by a fourth touch via push notification reusing Entrata's exact brand-guide copy ("Thanks for paying rent on time…").

Reward currencies

rmCash is the 1% cashback layer (1.0% on Visa rent payments). rmPoints is the loyalty-points currency layered in for resident engagement — earned at 1 point per $0.25 of rent paid. Both names sit comfortably in rmResident's native vocabulary ("rm" mirrors MRI's product prefix — rmResident, rmManager, rmCommercial).

ACH recovery path: rm Plus

Residents who pick Bank Account see a $9.99/mo rm Plus membership offer with 1% rmCash on ACH, 5% on local dining/home services/shopping, plus 7-day concierge. Recovers the lost cashback story for non-Visa payers.

Rewards wallet structure

Native-styled redeem options (Prepaid Visa, Travel, Pay rent with points, Gift card) sit at the top — matching the utilitarian list-style aesthetic of rmResident's transactions screen. Below that, an Amenify-powered service marketplace (Cleaning, Pet Care, Local Dining, etc.) gives residents a place to spend their rmCash.

Linked-card extension (screens 9–12)

Two entry points — an orange banner on the Approved screen and a tile in the Rewards Wallet — both route to the "Why link a card" explainer. From there: pick a card → confirmation moment → meet Maddie, the AI assistant who places orders and surfaces the cashback in real time ("$1.21 in rmCash, 5% back").

Single-wallet story

Per the partner decision, there's one currency across the whole flow: rmCash. Sarah sees the same wallet balance grow from her rent payment ($21.05) and from her Uber/Walmart/Domino's purchases — never two separate buckets. Reinforces a clean, white-labeled experience for Entrata to demo.

Maddie the AI assistant

Same AI persona as the LOFT and Homebody mockups, rebranded in rmResident's palette. The chat surface uses navy outgoing bubbles, the orange "R" mark on the earn callout, and the existing 5-tab Amenify nav (Home / Services / Rewards / Offers / Messages). Maddie is the "everywhere I spend" wedge — natural language ordering + rmCash earning in one moment.
