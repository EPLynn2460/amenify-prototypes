Design notes

The Homebody flow mirrors the LOFT structure (eight screens, Visa happy path, ACH alternate path, rewards wallet endpoint) and rebrands every reward surface in Homebody's own visual language — forest green + lime, editorial Fraunces serif, plant photography, and the existing Homebody Cash™ trademark already in Entrata's brand guide.

Triple reinforcement of the reward

The $21.05 + 8,420 points figure appears three times: in the inline offer on Payment Method, in the Summary callout, and again in the Approved screen. Followed by a fourth touch via push notification reusing Entrata's exact brand-guide copy ("Thanks for paying rent on time…").

Reward currencies

Homebody Cash™ is the 1% cashback layer (1.0% on Visa rent payments). Homebody Points are the existing 59,609-balance currency already shown in Homebody's app — earned at 1 point per $0.25 of rent paid. Both names sit comfortably in Homebody's native vocabulary.

ACH recovery path: Homebody+

Residents who pick Bank Account see a $9.99/mo Homebody+ membership offer with 1% Homebody Cash on ACH, 5% on local dining/home services/shopping, plus 7-day concierge. Recovers the lost cashback story for non-Visa payers.

Rewards wallet structure

Native Homebody redeem options (Prepaid Visa, Travel, Pay rent with points, Gift card) sit at the top — exactly as in Entrata's existing Rewards screen. Below that, an Amenify-powered service marketplace (Cleaning, Pet Care, Local Dining, etc.) gives residents a place to spend their Homebody Cash.

Linked-card extension (screens 9–12)

Two entry points — a lime banner on the Approved screen and a tile in the Rewards Wallet — both route to the "Why link a card" explainer. From there: pick a card → confirmation moment → meet Maddie, the Homebody AI assistant who places orders and surfaces the cashback in real time ("$1.21 in Homebody Cash, 5% back").

Single-wallet story

Per the partner decision, there's one currency across the whole flow: Homebody Cash™. Sarah sees the same wallet balance grow from her rent payment ($21.05) and from her Uber/Walmart/Domino's purchases — never two separate buckets. Reinforces a clean, white-labeled experience for Entrata to demo.

Maddie the AI assistant

Same AI persona as the source mockup, rebranded in Homebody's palette. The chat surface uses Homebody forest-green outgoing bubbles, the lime "h" mark on the earn callout, and the existing 5-tab Homebody nav (Home / Services / Rewards / Offers / Messages). Maddie is the "everywhere I spend" wedge — natural language ordering + Homebody Cash earning in one moment.
